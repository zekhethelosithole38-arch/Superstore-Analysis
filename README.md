# Superstore-Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the **Superstore dataset** to uncover insights into sales, profit, and customer behavior and understanding which products, regions, categories and customer segments they should target or avoid.

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final/data  
- Contains information on:
  - Orders (Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date)
  - Customer details (Segment, Region)
  - Product details (Category, Sub-Category)
  - Financials (Sales, Profit, Discount, Quantity)

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries
- Power BI 

## 🚀 Results & Insights
- Discounts often reduce profit margins despite boosting sales volume  
- Technology and Office Supplies are consistently profitable categories  
- The West region shows strong sales but lower profit margins compared to the East  

## 📈 Visualizations
Includes:
- KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Sales, Profit Margin  
- Sales by region, sub-category and segment
- Profits for each product, discount (%) and region
